How to play

Climb from the start word at the bottom to the goal word at the top by changing one letter at a time. Every step has to be a real four letter word.

coldcordcardwardwarm

Tap a tile, then tap a letter. Each move lifts the water one rung, and so does each hint. Reach the goal before the water tops the ladder. The ladder always has three rungs to spare beyond the shortest route.

Undo steps you back down but the water stays where it is. Everyone gets the same ladder each day: short early in the week, tallest on the weekend.

On a keyboard, type a letter to change the selected tile, use the arrow keys to pick a tile, Backspace to undo, and ? for a hint.
